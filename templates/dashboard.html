Scaling roadmap

Phase 1: Website bots Deploy and customize bots on business websites with branded widgets and templates.
Phase 2: Knowledge base + AI Turn websites into a business knowledge base and add retrieval-based answers later.
Phase 3: Conversations + leads Capture leads, store chats, and route valuable conversations to humans or CRM flows.
Phase 4: WhatsApp + Telegram + API Expand the same bot logic across the channels customers already use every day.
Phase 5: Multi-tenant SaaS Support teams, agencies, billing, analytics, and white-label deployments at scale.
